## Historical role

## Preservation policy

- Preserve exercises, notebooks, class ordering, examples, and the historical curriculum map.
- Do not treat the 23-class outline as the current official syllabus or as a verified record of what was actually taught in every offering.
- Do not upgrade libraries, rerun notebooks, replace datasets, or rewrite the course around current tools by default.
- Preserve attribution and teaching context when reusing an exercise elsewhere.

## Interpretation boundary

This repository is not the authoritative current FCEN course site, current curriculum, maintained textbook, or supported notebook environment. Some topics may be aspirational, draft, duplicated, or tied to an older offering.

## Revival rule

Reuse requires a current instructor or course consumer and a bounded selection of materials. Before editing, identify the offering, learning objective, exact notebook/exercise, environment, and acceptance check. Current teaching work should normally occur in the active course repository while citing this archive where useful.
